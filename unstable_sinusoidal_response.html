What “frequency response” means here

H(jω) gives the gain and phase of this special sinusoidal particular solution. For the causal unstable system, h(t) = eatu(t) has no ordinary Fourier transform; its Laplace ROC is Re(s) > a. The value H(jω) is the algebraic evaluation of the rational transfer function, outside that ROC. It is not an attracting steady-state response.

Why feedback is needed in practice

Exact cancellation needs exact dynamics and an exact initial state. Initial errors, model errors and disturbances excite the unstable mode. Feedback is used to stabilise the dynamics and maintain the desired behaviour despite these imperfections. The matched initial condition alone does not stabilise the system.

DC height = c₀. Other heights = 2|cₖ|, the peak cosine amplitudes. Negative-frequency lines are their complex conjugates.

The model uses xn = max(e−Δt/(RC)xn−1, vin,n − Vγ), with 8192 samples per input period. This converges to the continuous-time decaying-maximum model. R = 10 kΩ. The capacitor starts at zero only in startup mode. Spectra and Fourier sums always use the periodic steady-state waveform. Animation advances in input periods, independently of the physical frequency. Space: play/pause. ← / →: step. No network connection or installation is needed.

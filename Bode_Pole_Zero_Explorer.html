From roots to straight-line sketches

Real factors change the magnitude slope by ±20 dB/decade; conjugate pairs change it by ±40. Pair phase transitions use ωn10±|ζ|, as in the notes. Select a factor and choose Selected to compare its own exact and approximate curves.

Frequency and time responses

Choose Bode, Time response, and Vector geometry. Vector geometry sweeps jω up the imaginary axis and builds the exact magnitude and phase plots from the distances and angles of poles and zeros. The time plot always uses the total transfer function with zero initial conditions. For unstable poles, H(jω) need not represent a steady-state experiment from rest. Coincident pole and zero factors cancel algebraically; K is not generally the DC gain.

Sinusoidal steady state

yss(t) = M sin(ωt + φ)
M = 1/√[(1 − LCω²)² + (RCω)²],  φ = −atan2(RCω, 1 − LCω²)

Switching transient (zero-state)

ytr(t) = −yss(0) g0(t) − ẏss(0) g1(t)
Cancels the initial value and initial slope of the sinusoidal particular solution

Initial-condition response (zero-input)

yIC(t) = VC(0−) g0(t) + [iL(0−)/C] g1(t)
g₀(0) = 1, ġ₀(0) = 0; g₁(0) = 0, ġ₁(0) = 1

Total capacitor voltage

y(t) = yss(t) + ytr(t) + yIC(t)
y(0⁺) = VC(0⁻),  ẏ(0⁺) = iL(0⁻)/C

What to notice: unlike the RC circuit, the RLC transient can oscillate, and the rate of decay depends on the damping ratio ζ. The switching transient belongs to the response to the input with zero initial conditions; the initial-condition response is the separate response caused by energy already stored in the capacitor and inductor. Both contributions use the same natural modes.

Initial-condition contribution

Total capacitor voltage

y(t) = yss(t) + ytr(t) + yIC(t)
What to notice: the switching transient cancels the steady-state term at t = 0, so y(0+) = VC(0−). Both decaying terms have the same time constant τ, but different amplitudes and physical origins. The input has a fixed amplitude of 1 V.

No ordinary Fourier transform outside the ROC.
Essential distinction: Neither original growing signal has an ordinary Fourier transform (σ = 0). For σ > 1, the weighted signal e−σtx(t) is absolutely integrable and has an ordinary Fourier transform. At the boundary σ = 1, the usual integral does not converge, but a generalised Fourier transform exists and contains Dirac impulses and principal-value terms. The arrows shown at the boundary are symbolic, not a magnitude spectrum.
